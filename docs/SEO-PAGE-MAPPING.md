## Location strategy

- Use Bangalore and Bengaluru naturally.
- Focus initially on the main Bangalore service area rather than neighbourhood landing pages.
- Do not state that a service is available in Whitefield, HSR Layout or other neighbourhoods until
  service coverage is confirmed.
- No dedicated location pages should be published from the area-name list alone.

## Technical page requirements

- Preserve one descriptive H1 per page.
- Keep unique titles and descriptions under approximately 60 / 160 characters where practical.
- Keep canonical URLs dynamic and route-specific.
- Preserve existing service FAQ structured data and add BreadcrumbList only when the site URL is
  configured.
- Add Article/BlogPosting structured data only to actual blog detail pages.
- Add sitemap entries for all indexable service and blog routes.
- Do not add FAQPage markup to pages whose visible FAQ content is not present.
