## Competitor and SERP observations

The broad-match searches were dominated by furniture retailers and marketplaces, not specialist
restoration providers. That suggests a useful gap: specialist pages that answer structural
problems, restoration suitability, material choices and the actual repair process may better match
problem and commercial-investigation intent than a furniture-retail landing page.

The existing named competitors should be checked directly in Google search before implementation
copy is finalised:

- Karnataka Sofa Repairs
- The Fix Nation
- Local Bangalore furniture repair specialists
- Local polishing and restoration providers
- Justdial, UrbanClap-style marketplaces and Google Business profiles

No competitor rank, traffic, authority or result position is claimed in this document.

## Opportunities

1. Use `/services/furniture-repair` as the strongest structural-repair conversion page.
2. Split sofa upholstery and general furniture repair clearly so each page has a distinct intent.
3. Add a homeowner-focused furniture-restoration homepage section rather than broad marketing copy.
4. Keep Bengaluru as a natural variant of Bangalore; do not create dozens of duplicate area pages.
5. Build useful comparison and problem content in blogs rather than repeating service descriptions.
