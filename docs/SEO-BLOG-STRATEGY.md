Date: 2026-11-26

## Publication policy

Blog posts should answer a clear customer question and add useful detail. Do not publish thin
articles solely to increase page count. The initial strategy prioritises guides that support the
existing service pages and enquiry journey.

## Required internal links in each blog

Every blog should include:

- One contextual link to the relevant service page.
- One or two links to related blogs.
- One link to `/contact` or an enquiry prompt.
- Descriptive anchor text rather than repeated exact-match phrases.
- FAQ content only when the article answers genuine customer questions.

## Content requirements

- Unique SEO title and meta description.
- Single H1 matching the topic.
- A clear H2/H3 hierarchy.
- Practical examples and a direct answer in the introduction.
- Honest guidance on when a piece may or may not be suitable for restoration.
- No invented prices, timelines, project totals or guarantees.
- Bangalore/Bengaluru only where locally relevant.
- Contextual links to the correct existing service pages.
